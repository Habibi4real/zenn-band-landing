Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 9, 2026

Your privacy matters. This policy explains how Zenn handles information when you use our AI assistant on your phone, on a compatible smartwatch, or through our website.

1. About Zenn

Zenn is a voice-first AI assistant available through its mobile application, compatible wearable devices, website, and related services (together, the “Services”). Zenn helps you ask questions, manage tasks, work with connected applications, and review actions before they are completed when confirmation is required.

This Privacy Policy explains what personal data we process, why we process it, when information may be shared, and the choices and rights available to you. “Zenn,” “we,” “us,” and “our” refer to the operator of the Zenn Services. For privacy requests, contact contact.zennapp@gmail.com.

2. Information We Collect and Process

Account and profile information

When you register or use Zenn, we process information such as your email address, name or username, authentication identifiers, profile settings, language, time zone, and preferences. Authentication is provided through Supabase Auth; we do not need to receive your password in readable form to operate your account.

Conversations, voice commands, and files

We process the text of your requests, conversation history, generated answers, voice transcriptions, task instructions, confirmations, and the results of actions. If you use audio features, Zenn or supported device speech services may process microphone audio to recognize speech or provide a spoken reply. If you choose to attach photos, documents, or videos, their contents or extracted parts may be processed to answer your request. Video attachments may include selected frames rather than full video analysis, depending on the feature.

Connected accounts and task information

If you connect a third-party service, such as Gmail, Google Calendar, Google Drive, or another supported integration, Zenn may access the specific information authorized by you and needed for the actions you request. Depending on the permissions granted and the task, that may include email content and metadata, calendar events, file names and contents, contacts, messages, or action results. Zenn may store necessary task state, execution history, pending approvals, and related conversation context.

Device and technical information

We process information needed to operate and secure the Services, which may include device and smartwatch identifiers, pairing status, notification tokens if enabled, operating system and app version, connection information, timestamps, IP addresses processed by infrastructure providers, and diagnostic or error information.

Website, waitlist, and support

If you join the waitlist, request updates, or contact us, we process your email address and any information you choose to submit. Website hosting providers may also process routine connection and security logs.

3. How We Use Personal Information

We use information to:

  • Create, authenticate, manage, and secure your account and paired devices.
  • Understand requests, transcribe speech, generate responses, and perform authorized tasks.
  • Connect to services you select and retrieve or update information according to your permissions.
  • Maintain conversations, preferences, memory settings, reminders, and task progress where available.
  • Request and record confirmation for actions that require your approval.
  • Deliver requested notifications, service communications, and optional waitlist updates.
  • Diagnose failures, prevent misuse, maintain security, and improve reliability.
  • Respond to support requests and comply with applicable legal obligations.

4. Artificial Intelligence and Voice Processing

Zenn uses AI services, including OpenAI, to interpret requests, create responses, process eligible attachments, and provide speech-related functions. Information relevant to your request may be transmitted to an AI provider for processing. This can include your prompt, recent conversation context, relevant content returned by a connected service, attachments, and voice recordings or transcriptions when required for the feature you use.

On compatible smartwatches, speech recognition or spoken output may also be performed locally or through the device’s available speech services, subject to their own settings and privacy practices. The microphone is used for an activated voice interaction; any ongoing voice session should be ended or muted using the available controls.

AI-generated results may be inaccurate. Zenn uses permission and confirmation controls for supported actions, but you should review important information and consequential actions before approving them. Zenn does not sell your conversation content or connected-account data.

5. Connected Apps and Google User Data

Connecting an external account is optional. When you authorize a connection, Zenn receives access only within the scopes granted to the app. We use that access to deliver the user-facing actions and features you request, maintain the connection, and protect it from unauthorized use. Some task outputs and limited source content may be reflected in your Zenn conversation or execution history.

For Google services, Zenn’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs is intended to comply with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements where applicable. Google user data is not used for personalized advertising, sold to data brokers, or used to train general-purpose AI models. Access by people is limited to circumstances allowed by applicable rules, such as your express consent, security investigations, compliance with law, or necessary internal operations.

You may disconnect supported integrations in Zenn or revoke their permissions through the third-party service. Disconnecting an integration stops future authorized access but may not automatically remove information already included in previous conversations or action records; you can separately request deletion of that data.

6. Legal Bases for Processing

Where the GDPR or similar data-protection laws apply, we rely on an appropriate legal basis for each activity. These may include: performance of a contract or steps requested by you to provide Zenn; legitimate interests in security, service reliability, fraud prevention, and support, balanced against your rights; your consent where required for optional communications or particular permissions; and compliance with legal obligations. You can withdraw consent where processing is based on consent, without affecting processing that was lawful before withdrawal.

7. Sharing with Service Providers and Others

We may share or make data accessible only as needed to operate a requested feature, including with:

  • Supabase, for authentication, database services, and backend infrastructure.
  • OpenAI, for AI inference, transcription, and speech functions used by Zenn.
  • Hosting and infrastructure providers, such as Vercel and Railway, where used to deliver the website, application services, or automation workers.
  • Browser and automation providers, where a feature you activate requires a supported remote browser or similar tool.
  • Connected third-party services, such as Google services or other integrations that you authorize, in order to read information or carry out your requested action.
  • Notification delivery providers and security or operational service providers, when relevant to enabled features.

We may also disclose information when legally required, to protect users or the Services, or as part of a legitimate organizational transaction subject to applicable safeguards. We do not sell personal information.

8. Storage, Retention, and Deletion

We keep account and profile information for as long as necessary to maintain your account. Conversation history, memory, connected-account metadata, tasks, confirmations, and execution records may remain associated with your account until deleted, until no longer needed for the purpose collected, or until removal is required by applicable law. Temporary files, technical logs, and backups may have different retention periods depending on their purpose and the providers involved. We seek to minimize unnecessary retention.

After a verified deletion request, we take steps to delete or anonymize personal information associated with your account, subject to information we must retain for security, fraud prevention, dispute resolution, or legal obligations. Some copies may remain temporarily in backups or third-party systems according to documented retention and deletion processes. Information held independently by a connected provider is governed by that provider’s policies.

To request deletion, see Delete account or email contact.zennapp@gmail.com with the subject “Delete my Zenn account,” preferably from the email address linked to your Zenn account. We may need to verify that you own the account before acting.

9. Security

We use technical and organizational measures designed to protect data, including authentication, access restrictions, and safeguards for connected-account credentials. Stored third-party access tokens are protected by backend controls. Smartwatch pairing uses authorization and revocable device credentials. No transmission or storage system is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

10. Your Rights and Choices

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or receive a copy of your information; object to or restrict certain processing; withdraw consent; and lodge a complaint with a data-protection authority. Where processing is based on legitimate interests, you may object subject to applicable exceptions.

You can also choose whether to connect third-party accounts, enable optional notifications, use voice input, or pair a wearable device. To exercise privacy rights, contact contact.zennapp@gmail.com. If you are in the European Economic Area, you may contact your local supervisory authority about a concern.

11. International Data Transfers

Some providers and connected services operate in countries outside your country of residence, including outside the European Economic Area. Where legally required, international transfers are subject to appropriate safeguards, such as adequacy decisions or contractual protections. The specific locations and safeguards can vary with the services and integrations you choose.

12. Children’s Privacy

Zenn is not designed for children. Users must meet the minimum age and any consent requirements applicable to the Services in their jurisdiction. If you believe a child has provided personal information without the required authorization, contact us so we can investigate and take appropriate steps.

13. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as Zenn develops or legal requirements change. We will update the “Last updated” date and provide additional notice when required by applicable law. The policy available at this address will describe the current practices for the applicable version of the Services.

QUESTIONS

What we know so far.

Do I need the Band to use Zenn? +

No. Zenn is being developed as an app first. The Band is a future way to reach the same assistant.

When will Zenn Band be available? +

We have not announced a release date. Join the waitlist to hear when there is news.

Can I buy or preorder it now? +

Not yet. The Band is a concept preview and the waitlist is for updates, not a purchase or reservation.

Will it work with my phone? +

Compatibility details will be shared as the product develops.

What are the final specifications? +

Hardware details, colors, battery life and pricing are not final. We will publish confirmed information before launch.

What happens if Zenn needs my approval? +

Zenn's permission settings and confirmation flow remain part of the experience. We will share the exact Band interaction as it develops.

Delete Your Zenn Account

You can request deletion of your Zenn account and associated personal data at any time.

How to delete your account

To request deletion of your account, email us at contact.zennapp@gmail.com from the email address associated with your Zenn account. Use the subject line Delete my Zenn account.

Account deletion is currently handled through this email request process. A direct in-app account deletion option is not yet available in the current test version of Zenn.

We may contact you to verify account ownership before processing your request. Please do not send your password or other sensitive sign-in credentials by email.

What data is deleted

When your account deletion request has been verified and completed, we delete or anonymize personal information associated with your Zenn account, subject to the exceptions below. This may include:

  • Your account profile, email association, and authentication account.
  • Your conversation history, messages, and saved AI responses.
  • Your saved memories, preferences, personalization settings, and onboarding information.
  • Your reminders, active tasks, pending actions, and related execution history.
  • Files and images you uploaded to Zenn, to the extent stored or controlled by Zenn.
  • Your connected-app authorization records and integration settings stored by Zenn.
  • Paired smartwatch or device records, notification tokens, and app preferences associated with your account.

Deleting your Zenn account does not itself delete emails, calendar events, documents, or other information held independently in external services you connected to Zenn.

What may be kept

Some limited information may be retained when necessary for security, fraud prevention, legal compliance, tax or accounting obligations, dispute resolution, or to document and fulfill a deletion request.

If purchases or subscriptions are offered through Google Play or another payment provider, payment and transaction records maintained by that provider are subject to its own retention and deletion policies.

Residual information in backups or service logs may take additional time to be removed under the relevant infrastructure provider's retention procedures, unless a longer period is legally required. We do not currently specify a universal 90-day backup deletion deadline.

Processing time

We aim to process verified account deletion requests within 30 days. If additional time is legally permitted and required, or we need information to confirm account ownership, we will contact you when appropriate.

Need help?

For questions about account deletion or personal data, contact Zenn at contact.zennapp@gmail.com.

You can also read our Privacy Policy and Terms & Conditions.

Questions, answered.

What is Zenn? +

Zenn is a personal AI assistant that helps you keep context, organize work and act across the apps you connect.

What is Zenn Band? +

Zenn Band is a wearable product concept designed to bring Zenn closer to everyday life.

How will I use it? +

We are developing the experience and will share confirmed details with the waitlist.

Can I connect my apps? +

Zenn supports connected apps. Availability and capabilities depend on each service and your permissions.

When will Zenn Band be available? +

We have not announced a launch date. Join the waitlist for updates.

How much will it cost? +

Pricing has not been announced.

How does Zenn handle my data? +

You choose which apps to connect and can review memory and permissions in Zenn. Band-specific details will be published before launch.
